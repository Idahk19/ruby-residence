Ruby Residence is a residential apartment system used to manage tenants, rent records, and communication between residents and management. It provides a structured way to organize housing information and keep track of daily apartment operations.

How the Residence Works

Tenants are registered into the system and assigned apartment units. Monthly rent records and updates are maintained by the administration.

Communication between tenants and management is handled through notices and issue reporting.

Apartment Overview

Key areas managed within Ruby Residence

Tenant Records

Information about tenants and their assigned units is stored for reference.

Rent Tracking

Monthly rent status and payment records are maintained.

Residence Notices

Updates from management are shared with all tenants.
