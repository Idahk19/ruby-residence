Ruby Residence

Ruby Residence is a residential apartment system used to manage tenants, rent records, and communication between residents and management.

The system provides a structured way to organize housing information and support daily apartment operations within the residence.

Tenant Portal Admin Portal

How the Residence Works

Tenants are registered into the residence system and assigned apartment units managed by administration.

Monthly rent records, notices, and maintenance issues are handled within the system to support communication between management and residents.

Apartment Overview

Key areas managed within Ruby Residence

Tenant Records

Information about tenants and assigned apartment units is maintained for management reference.

Rent Tracking

Monthly rent records and payment updates are organized within the apartment system.

Residence Notices

Important announcements and updates are shared with all residents through the platform.
